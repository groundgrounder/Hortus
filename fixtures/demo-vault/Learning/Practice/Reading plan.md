# Reading plan

Read with a question, then leave a useful trail for your future self.

1. Pick a branch from [[Learning map]].
2. Write down what you expect to learn.
3. Read for twenty minutes.
4. Close the source and follow the [[Study loop]].
5. Record one uncertainty in [[Weekly review]].
